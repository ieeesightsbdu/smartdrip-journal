## 📝 End of Day Wrap-Up
- **What got done:** We successfully connected a sonar sensor , a  photoresistor and a DHT22 to our second Esp32 . And based on the soil moisture reading,the Webapp could on and off our pump  automatically. 

- **Blockers / Unfinished:**Even though we could get the sonar sensor to read the data from the system successfully , it was not providing value continuously. 
- **Key Learnings / Notes:** After further testing , we figured that the reason  behind this instability of sonar sensor data might be because of the height of our tank . The depth of our tank is approximately 30cm and minimum measured distance by the sensor was 22cm. As the difference between them are too small ,the sensor can not operate properly. So we are considering to use alternative sensor  or another method to measure the water level of tank . Our next priority are to set the environmental scoring algorithm on our Webapp , calibrate the light level and water level . Then manually on/off pump through the Webapp . And also find a reliable way to measure water level of the tank .
